mumbai city-the city of dreams

Mumbai, the financial capital of India, is a city where dreams take flight and opportunities never stop. Located on the western coast of Maharashtra, Mumbai is famous for its fast-paced lifestyle, diverse culture, historical landmarks, Bollywood industry and economic growth. Known as the "City of Dreams", it attracts millions of people who come here with hopes of building a better future.
